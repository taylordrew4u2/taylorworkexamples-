## Scope of the proof

- A current Apple listing establishes App Store availability. It does not establish downloads, revenue, client results, feature-by-feature runtime validation, or that the portfolio artwork shows the current released version.
- Apple's My Gig Calendar and Open Micer Timer listings include compatibility for Apple-silicon Macs, but explicitly state **“Not verified for macOS.”** They are not used as proof of tested native Mac distribution. Native Mac and cross-platform desktop source projects are described separately.
- The public [Open Micer Timer repository](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/Open-Micer-Timer) describes iPhone, iPad, and Mac source targets. Its newer source and product artwork do not make every described capability a verified feature of the App Store release.
- Existing source and capture disclosures remain applicable: BitBinder's development captures use fictional material; supplied banners are product artwork; My Gig Calendar's native captures use sample gigs; Timer's retained native countdown views are dated simulator captures.
- Public copy can explain App Store delivery, native app work, database-backed web applications, and desktop software through these products and their linked evidence. No years of experience, senior job title, commercial customer relationship, or performance result is inferred.
