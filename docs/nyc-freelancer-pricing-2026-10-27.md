# NYC independent freelancer pricing — October 27, 2026

## Current instruction and decision

The owner rejected the former $9.50 hourly rate and its overseas budget-company comparisons. The current instruction is to compare 4u Digital with independent developers actually based in New York City and position the business a little below appropriate local comparators. This document supersedes the decisions in [the earlier global comparison](./competitive-pricing-update-2026-10-27.md), which remains a historical research record. It does not establish the lowest price charged by every NYC freelancer.

Research used free public search, provider websites, and provider-authored professional profiles on October 27, 2026. No paid tools, accounts, quote requests, or outreach were used. Published asking prices are not verified transaction prices. Provider location and individual working arrangements were checked separately from service-area marketing pages.

**Separately agreed additional work: $75/hour.** This is 6.25% below Good Foundations Studio's published $80/hour sole-proprietor tier and 16.67% below Organic Web Design's $90/hour non-rush rate. These services are relevant local reference points, not identical work or a market average. Some verified local providers advertise lower rates for narrower work; $75 is not a promise to undercut all NYC freelancers. Included guarantee changes and existing contracted support remain included.

The hour figures above are internal assumptions used to sanity-check the decision. They are not measured past delivery data, time estimates for an unspecified client, client entitlements, included-hour allowances, deadlines, or delivery guarantees. Discovery, design, testing, deployment and support can materially change effort. Confirm the actual requirements, scope, complete price and timeline in each client's written agreement before building.

The software and native-app budgets are particularly dependent on scope. This review found no reliable fixed-price native Swift/Apple package from a verified NYC independent that establishes a comparable market floor. A micro-app, a prototype, a full production service and a native Apple app are not interchangeable products. Do not describe the $6,000 Apple starting budget as a researched lowest-local-price discount.

## Retained commitments and terms

- The two website packages remain the existing limited offer for the first ten eligible NYC projects agreed by November 30, 2026, subject to scope and capacity. Their scope, eligibility, payment/deposit terms and extra three-month content-change benefit are unchanged.
- Every project keeps the full development-fee refund promise within 30 days after the completed product launches, with reasonable changes included during that period.
- The optional six-month debugging/support arrangement retains changes at 50% off the agreed standard rate. **$37.50/hour is only the example when that particular agreement uses a $75/hour standard rate.** It does not reprice existing agreements or turn included support into an extra charge.
- Clients retain ownership of 100% of their custom code and code/deployment handover. Client work and code stay out of the public portfolio without permission.
- There is no required 4u Digital maintenance subscription. Third-party running costs are separate and agreed before building. Contracts remain tailored to each client's needs and comfort.
- Existing signed agreements and promised included work are not changed by this pricing revision.

## Limits, excluded comparisons and review

Overseas budget agencies, generic NYC-targeted landing pages without local individual evidence, staffing bill rates, salary tables, unverified marketplace snippets and third-party city-average SEO articles do not set this decision. The former global-company research remains available only to explain the superseded decision.

4u Digital's cost base, available billable hours, tax/insurance costs, actual delivery time, guarantee claims and ongoing support burden are not established by this research. The adopted prices are not a profitability calculation. Review actual delivery and included support time, realized earnings, accepted quotes and inquiry quality after projects; reassess before accepting a scope that cannot be sustained. Do not automatically chase lower advertisements or weaken the existing guarantee to compensate for pricing.

Public copy should lead with service scope, clear price expectations and direct work with the developer. It should not claim to be the cheapest, below every NYC freelancer, equivalent in scope to every named provider, or guaranteed to deliver results because a comparison was researched.
