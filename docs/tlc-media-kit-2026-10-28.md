## What the visuals establish

The interfaces show a local application demo with sample content and fictional appointment data. Promotional photography is AI-generated conceptual wellness imagery, not photographs of actual premises, staff, or treatments. The two silent videos combine project captures and artwork; they are not recordings of real customer activity.

The application supports treatment pages, appointment requests, owner review, status changes, private notes, and editable website content. Requests remain pending until the owner accepts them; submitting a request does not reserve synchronized calendar availability. Prepared email replies open the owner's mail application. Online payments, automatic email delivery, and calendar synchronization are not implemented.

Persistent PostgreSQL storage and Vercel Blob image uploads require configuration. Without the database configuration, the application uses an in-memory fallback that loses changes on restart; these captures use that fallback. The showcase does not claim a production deployment, real bookings, or measured client outcomes.
