# Free lead tracking

Open `lead-log-template.csv` in Numbers, LibreOffice, or another spreadsheet you already use. Keep the filled-in file private; it is an internal business record, not website content.

Add one row per genuine enquiry. Record the originating page from an email draft's “Source page” field, or the source the client tells you. For BriefBooth bookings, record the confirmed booking date and ask where they found 4u Digital if the source is unknown. Do not infer a completed booking from an outbound link click.

Once a week, count enquiries, consultations, proposals, and agreed projects. Compare the actual source pages and referrals behind those outcomes. Leave unknown sources blank rather than guessing. A small number of enquiries is useful feedback, not enough evidence for a conversion-rate claim.

This adds no visitor tracker, paid reporting service, or new booking questions to the portfolio.
