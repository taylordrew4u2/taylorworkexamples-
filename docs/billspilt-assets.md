## Source and capture context

The [pinned README](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/Bill-Spilt/blob/a45aad25f6274313a3c82329a87bba196f860aae/README.md) describes BillSpilt as a shared-expense app for roommates and labels its animated demonstration as a local build with sample data. The static captures depict the same Maple Street Flat example household. Its privacy documentation distinguishes household admins, who see all figures, from members, who see their own share, balances, and payments. The balances and statistics images are household admin views, not evidence of ordinary member access to everyone’s finances.

`hero.png`, `features.png`, and `how-it-works.png` are composed repository promotional graphics with copy, backgrounds, and phone-framed interface imagery. Their gallery entries are labeled **Repository product artwork** and use `sourceKind: "repository-product-artwork"`. They are separate from the five unframed app-interface captures in `docs/screenshots/`, labeled **Repository app capture** with `sourceKind: "repository-app-capture"`. These are existing public repository materials, not owner-supplied attachments or freshly captured portfolio runtime evidence.

No app session, offline synchronization, privacy rule, export, Venmo/Cash App interaction, ledger mutation, or actual money transfer was exercised during this asset update. Mark-as-paid controls and illustrative settlement confirmations do not establish that money moved. The README’s animated demo was reviewed as context but was not downloaded or added to the portfolio.

The intended showcase order is product-overview cover, five interface captures, then the how-it-works and features graphics: eight views in total. Existing project identity and authentic icon remain unchanged. The surrounding Built4U page accents remain monochrome; blue, purple, pink, cream, and other colors within original BillSpilt images remain unchanged.
