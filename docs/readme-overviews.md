At initial refresh, each overview was the first **Product overview** frame, labeled **Repository product artwork**. The sixteen earlier gallery frames retain their images, captions, source links, and ordering after the new cover. The current curated selection publishes only SobStage, Pins & Needles, and The Trip Handler from this table. BleepKit, DicType, and RoleCall remain internal evidence and their exclusive artwork, icons, routes, and gallery files are excluded from the distribution. Earlier views remain available for retained projects. The opening uses the Pins & Needles and SobStage overviews and links to their complete project pages.

These overview composites are presentation artwork. They do not establish a new native export, dictation session, production transaction, or physical microphone test. The existing DicType capture still documents onboarding without microphone/speech permissions; SobStage's older captures still use virtual microphones, and physical microphone validation remains pending. RoleCall's development screens use a fictional screenplay, while Trip Handler's app captures use seeded demo trip data. Artwork performance figures are not portfolio measurements.

The page's branding remains black, white, and gray. Original project colors are preserved, including colors already present in repository artwork. Complete landscape product artwork uses its source aspect ratio, while ordinary app captures retain the existing gallery layout.
