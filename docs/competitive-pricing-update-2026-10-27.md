## Primary sources and scope differences

Client supplies content; one revision; optional $29/month care. Custom functionality is separately quoted. 4u Digital retains its own broader three-month content-change benefit and refund promises.
- [SilkaWeb pricing](https://www.silkaweb.com/Home/Pricing?lang=en): $499–$999 business website, up to six pages, basic appointment/customer dashboard, email notifications and optional login/database. The minimum does not establish every complete workflow at that price; $475 intentionally undercuts the published entry floor while 4u Digital keeps its existing one-task scope. [FAQ](https://www.silkaweb.com/Home/FAQ?lang=en) confirms files owned after payment and maintenance optional.
- [Devzey pricing](https://www.devzey.com/pricing): USD per-project web app entry $600, with database and admin dashboard; its calculator lists bespoke design and accounts as separately priced options. Its mobile MVP is cross-platform and is not native Swift evidence. Its automation entry also starts at $600.
- [Pineywoods Web iOS apps](https://pineywoodsweb.com/apps): simple apps from $999, explicitly native Swift, device testing and App Store submission, subject to a written scoped quote. This establishes an iPhone comparison, not a separate Mac-app market minimum. 4u Digital applies one approved entry price to its one-platform Apple category.
- [GrahAI custom software](https://www.grahaisystems.com/custom-software-development): single-purpose tool from $599 with a simple staff screen, CSV import and two revision rounds. Its $199 CSV merge and $399 order sync are narrower individual jobs, not a comparable complete staff tool. Its generic $99 headline lacks the scoped tool package. These lower numbers were recorded rather than misrepresented as complete applications.
- [HireDeveloper](https://hiredeveloper.co.in/): India-based provider lists developer work from $10/hour and senior from $25/hour. $10 is the broader low-end support floor, not an equivalent senior NYC rate. The owner requested the lowest entry comparison without a geographic floor; $9.50 is therefore a deliberate budget-market rate and a major reduction from $70.

## Economics, alternatives, and review

Cost base, estimated hours, margin and willingness to pay are unknown. These reductions follow the owner's explicit instruction; they are not a profitability recommendation. The strongest alternative was to retain the previous scoped prices, which better insulate time and support costs. It was not selected because the owner expressly requested lower entry pricing. No existing guarantees were weakened to offset the reduction.

Review actual scoped hours, included revisions/support, realized hourly earnings, inquiry quality and accepted quotes before agreeing each project. Revisit prices if measured delivery/support cost makes them unsustainable, a comparator changes currency/scope/mandatory costs, or the owner requests a new positioning. Do not automatically chase future low advertisements or rewrite signed client contracts.
