# Freelancer comparison and mobile polish

Reviewed October 20, 2026. This is an editorial comparison of live public pages, including homepage views at 390 × 844 pixels. The observations describe visible content and interface choices. They are not measured conversion results, a performance benchmark, or evidence that another site attracts more inquiries than 4u Digital.

## Primary sources and observations

| Site | Observed pattern | Useful application and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Built by Denney](https://builtbydenney.co.uk/) | The phone layout shows a contact button at the top and a fixed bottom navigation bar with Menu, Work, Services, and Contact. Project descriptions explain the task before listing technologies. | Keep a clear consultation action reachable during a long page. Use practical project descriptions. A full bottom navigation bar and its animated presentation are unnecessary for this polish. |
| [Brad McAllister](https://bradmcallister.com/) | The phone hero presents work and contact actions. The homepage introduces three recent projects and links to the full portfolio. The [contact page](https://bradmcallister.com/contact) explains the initial conversation and proposal process alongside an external booking link. | Preserve 4u Digital’s existing hero choices and keep optional project details compact. Explain the booking handoff briefly beside the consultation link. The other developer’s commercial terms do not establish 4u Digital’s terms. |
| [John Carter](https://www.johncarter.co.uk/) | Service choices distinguish business websites, web apps, and online shops. Project titles describe deliverables, including a client portal and marketing website. Its phone menu contains many service categories. | Give an ordinary website request an obvious route to relevant service information. Keep navigation shorter than a full service directory. No need to reproduce its ratings, menu, or contact form. |

The live [4u Digital homepage](https://customtechbuilt4u.com/) already shows its consultation button and work link in the initial phone view. It also explains the direct relationship with the developer. The chosen polish keeps that opening and improves access to the next step further down the page.

## Chosen implementation focus

- Add a compact mobile consultation dock linking directly to [4u Digital on BriefBooth](https://briefbooth.com/book/4u-digital). Reserve space for it and account for phone safe areas so it does not cover content or controls. Keep the action labeled **Book a consultation**.
- Change the main homepage service-card actions from email drafts to the relevant service pages. Visitors can understand the website, web app, or app/software offer before deciding to contact 4u Digital. General project inquiry email remains available elsewhere.
- Add two short homepage FAQ answers: a straightforward business website can be a project on its own, and the client owns 100% of their custom code. Preserve the separate commitment that client work and code stay out of the public portfolio without permission.
- Make the BriefBooth handoff visible in brief text. Booking continues on the external page in a new tab; no appointment form, intake questions, or booking walkthrough belongs on 4u Digital.

Preserve the hero, genuine project images, selected project catalog, direct developer relationship, and the order of work, testimonials, and process. Keep the neutral black, white, and gray presentation. These changes use the existing site and booking destination, with no paid services or added analytics.

## Limits

The other sites publish their own availability, review, pricing, response-time, and delivery claims. Those claims were not independently verified and are not recommendations to adopt matching promises. No forms were submitted, messages sent, accounts created, or paid tools used during the comparison. The research establishes design choices worth considering; it does not establish a conversion improvement or guarantee new inquiries.
