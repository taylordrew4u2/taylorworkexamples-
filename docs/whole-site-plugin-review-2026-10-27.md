# Whole-site plugin review — October 27, 2026

The owner asked to use the relevant plugins across the entire 4u Digital site. The scope is practical client clarity and mobile usability, preserving the approved minimal monochrome design, original full-color project art, selected twelve projects, all nine supplied reviews, current prices and guarantees, business-only identity, direct BriefBooth booking, optional planner, and no paid services.

## Evidence and decisions

- BrandKit and Product & App Architect guidance informed a read-only check of the final generated brand, information architecture and release/evidence claims. All 52 canonical routes connect, with no broken local route or fragment targets. These are static findings, not claims about inquiries or conversion.
- Creator & Freelance Toolkit and HumanWriting guidance identified the practical copy/navigation corrections below. No marketplace, subscription, or template was added.
- Career Evidence Toolkit evidence discipline supported the existing three official Apple listings and the FreeScript/SobStage distinction. The earlier release in the same session added FreeScript to the optional desktop range proof while retaining its signed-but-not-notarized status and SobStage's pending physical microphone validation. No resume, private career workspace, personal biography, senior title, years-of-experience claim or invented business result was created.
- Marketing Council's decision framework favored correcting existing paths and ambiguities over adding more sections. The alternative of expanding the homepage, adding more actions, or rewriting every page had no demonstrated need and would conflict with the owner's minimal-site direction. Actual booking and inquiry outcomes remain unknown; local correctness and usability checks do not establish a conversion increase.
- The technical review resolved every local document/fragment, image/srcset, font URL and fingerprinted module import in the generated site, checked metadata/structured data, and confirmed all 96 original gallery files. Booking anchors use the exact approved destination, new-tab behavior and rel attributes. Static navigation fallbacks remain present.

## Changes made

1. The homepage's existing cost FAQ links to Pricing as well as the limited NYC website offer.
2. The Services comparison link now sits beside the four general choices and the quiet Pricing link. Each service detail also links to Pricing from its existing planning section.
3. Pricing distinguishes eligible fixed-scope website offers, starting app/software budgets and separately agreed hourly work. Every actual price and shared promise remains unchanged. Its meta description is shorter and specific.
4. The planner leads with businesses, organizations and general customer tasks. Every former performer/personal option remains available. Existing selection values and emailed answers are preserved; their array order is not a chronological commitment.
5. Header/footer logo anchors have at least 44px height without changing the original image dimensions.

## External tool checks and limits

Grow My Website's free public scan reported homepage 100/100, Services 96/100 and Pricing 96/100 before this patch. Its Pricing warning concerned a 190-character description; the revised description addresses that. The Services warning requested 600 words. No filler was added: Google's [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) states there is no required minimum or maximum word count. These scanner scores cover basic on-page checks, not rankings, traffic or leads.

Better Design's text checks found no insider-language or purpose-comprehension issue in the tested openings. Its Pricing opening passed; its homepage and combined Services/choices sample exceeded the tool's heuristic 15-second budget. That estimate is not observed user reading time. The approved homepage's two actions and explicit booking conditions were retained rather than removed to satisfy a word-count formula. The review is not represented as a universal comprehension pass.

Better Design completed geometry inspection of the live Services page at 390, 1440 and 1728px before changes. A separate skeptical pass rejected the apparent clipping failures: they were intentional 1px `.sr-only` new-tab notices, not missing visible content. Its generic spacing-scale and inferred-density suggestions did not demonstrate defects in the selected design. The small logo link targets were real and were enlarged. Original captures/reports retain these tool limitations; no clean automated visual-approval claim is made.

The VibeSEO project's harmless listing did not include 4u Digital. No unrelated personal-site analytics, keywords or settings were accessed or joined to the business. Website Auditor's actual-domain endpoint requires a paid subscription, so it was not run and no sample score is presented as this site's result. No paid keyword lookup, translation, content generation, image generation, outreach, external email, real booking, paid analytics or new third-party account was initiated.

## Verification boundary

Regression coverage checks keyboard/touch navigation from the cost FAQ and Services to Pricing, all six service planning links, 320/390px layouts, optional planner answers/drafts/uploads and simulated submission/retry, alongside the existing original-image, ownership, guarantee and no-JavaScript checks. Browser submission tests intercept the backend and external booking destination; they do not send a real client brief or appointment request. Manual views complement these tests. Actual email inbox delivery and completed appointments are not inferred.

Keep the change if clients can find their service, understand its price basis, and reach the correct booking destination. If genuine client feedback shows confusion, revise that specific path. Inquiry source context and actual booked consultations can inform that decision without adding paid tracking or promising attribution the portfolio cannot observe.
