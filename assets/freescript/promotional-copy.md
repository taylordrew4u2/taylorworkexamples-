## Launch announcement

Your story. Your page.

Meet FreeScript: a free, offline screenwriting app for Mac. Paste a pre-written script or import a text-based PDF, then keep writing in a cream-paper, dark-ink workspace. Navigate scenes, revise dialogue, save drafts locally, and export Fountain or PDF.

There is also a FreeScript companion for ChatGPT and Claude to format, revise, analyze, and export screenplay text. It is separate from your Mac library; move drafts between them with import and export.

Download for Mac: https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/FreeScript/releases/tag/v1.0.3

## Press boilerplate

FreeScript is a free Mac screenplay editor designed around familiar paper-and-ink writing. The desktop application works offline and stores drafts locally. It supports common screenplay elements, text-based PDF/Fountain imports, and PDF/Fountain/JSON exports. Its optional online companion provides screenplay tools inside ChatGPT and Claude. Source and releases are available on GitHub under the MIT license.
