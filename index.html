BEFORE WE START

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What if my idea isn’t finished?

That’s fine. Start with the problem you want to solve. We can work out the details together.

How is pricing worked out?

Pricing depends on scope, features, and complexity. We’ll agree what’s included and the price before building.

Does a consultation commit me to a project?

No. The consultation is a conversation about your needs and whether the project is a fit. Before any build, we’ll agree the scope, price, start date, and target launch in writing. See the NYC website offer and its terms ↗

What happens after launch?

We’ll discuss who maintains it and any hosting/service costs when defining the project.

What if I only need a website?

That’s a complete project too. I can build a straightforward site for your services, location, work, and contact details. We’ll choose the pages and features your business needs. Explore website services ↗

Who owns the code, and will my project be public?

All custom code I build for your project is 100% yours. Client work and code will never appear in my public portfolio without your permission. The projects shown here are selected personal projects.
