A FEW QUESTIONS

Before we start.

What if I don’t know exactly what I need?

Start with the problem. You don’t need a finished plan or technical knowledge. We’ll work out what’s useful for your business together.

How much will my project cost?

It depends on the features and scope. You’ll have a clear price and written agreement before building. See the NYC website offer for a smaller website starting point.

Can we meet online or in person?

First consultations are online. If you’re unsure what you need, or the software needs to fit a physical space or workflow, NYC visits are available by arrangement in Manhattan, Brooklyn, and parts of Queens.
